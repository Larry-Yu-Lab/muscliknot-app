# MuscliKnot

This is my app MuscliKnot! I made it to help people deal with muscle pain, stiffness, and knots.

I play sports and work out a lot, and whenever I got a random knot in my shoulder or lower back, it was annoying trying to describe it or find the right stretches online. I wanted something where you could literally just tap on a body model to show where it hurts and immediately get stretches and exercises to help fix it.

What the app does:
- Interactive body map: You can flip between the front and back of the body, zoom in, and place a marker right on the muscle that hurts.
- Different modes: You can choose between quick relief, warmups, yoga stretches, posture fixing, or strengthening.
- Step-by-step instructions: Shows you how to do each stretch properly and how long to hold it.
- Saved list: You can save stretches you like so you do not have to search for them again.
- History: Keeps track of your sessions and streaks so you can see your recovery over time.
- Extra stuff: Dark mode, multi-language support (English, Spanish, French, Chinese), and basic profile stats.

How I made it:
- React Native and Expo
- Supabase for the database
- TypeScript

How to run it:
1. Run "npm install"
2. Run "npx expo start"
3. Open it in the Expo Go app on your phone (scan the QR code) or press "i" for iOS simulator / "a" for Android emulator.
